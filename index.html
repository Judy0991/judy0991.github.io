Hello! I am Yueqi Jiang (姜月琦), a master’s student in Applied Mathematics and Computational Science at the University of Pennsylvania. I received my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Beijing Normal University.

My research lies at the intersection of biostatistics, causal inference, clinical research, and health data science. I am especially interested in developing statistically principled methods for real-world healthcare data, with current work spanning synthetic control methods with missing data, longitudinal clinical data, autism screening, medical imaging, and emerging applications of AI agents in healthcare.

I currently work with Prof. Jing Huang on methodological and clinical research at Penn and the Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia. Across my projects, I aim to connect rigorous statistical reasoning with questions that matter in practice: how to recover credible counterfactuals from incomplete data, how to evaluate clinical screening tools, and how to make computational models useful and interpretable for healthcare decisions.
